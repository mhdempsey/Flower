Quick Setup Guide

  1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations
  2. Click "New integration" and give it a name (e.g., "Florilege")
  3. Copy the "Internal Integration Token" and paste it above
  4. Open your Notion database and click "..." → "Add connections" → Select your integration
  5. Copy the database ID from the URL (32 characters) and paste it above
  6. Click "Save Settings" and refresh your new tab
